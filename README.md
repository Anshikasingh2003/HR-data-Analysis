# HR Analytics Dashboard

**Tools:** Excel (data cleaning, pivot tables, slicers, dashboard)

## Business question

Help an organisation understand employee attrition: who is leaving, from which department, and at what age and salary level.

## Data

1,473 employee records with 38 columns, including age group, department, job role, gender, salary slab, overtime, job satisfaction, years at company and attrition status.

## Approach

1. Cleaned the data and used grouping columns such as age group and salary slab.
2. Built pivot tables for headcount and attrition by age group, gender, department, salary slab and tenure.
3. Combined them into one interactive dashboard with slicers.

## Key results

| KPI | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Employees | 1,473 |
| Employees who left | 237 |
| Attrition rate | 16.1% |

## Insights

- Almost half of all leavers (116 of 237) are aged 26–35.
- Most leavers earn up to 5k a month (163 of 237), so attrition is concentrated in the lowest salary slab.
- Research & Development loses the most people (133), followed by Sales (92).
